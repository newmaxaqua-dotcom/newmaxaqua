Water tank deep cleaning

Our 5-step cleaning process.

  1. Inspection

    We assess the tank's condition and plan the safest cleaning approach.

  2. Draining

    The tank is fully drained to expose dirt, sludge and algae build-up.

  3. Deep cleaning

    Walls and floor are scrubbed and pressure-washed to remove every deposit.

  4. Sanitization

    Safe disinfectants eliminate bacteria and harmful contaminants.

  5. Refill & final check

    The tank is refilled and checked so every stored drop stays hygienic.

Questions

Which purifier is right for my home?

It depends on your water source and quality. We test your water first, then recommend an RO, UV, UF, alkaline or copper purifier from our 17 models.

How do you choose the filter vessel size?

From your water test. Higher impurity loads need larger vessels. Common sizes run from 10×54 to 14×65 inches.

Do you service what you install?

Yes. We offer AMCs, repairs and cartridge replacement from both the Kottayam and Ernakulam offices.

How much can I save with solar?

Up to 80% on electricity bills. Panels last decades with little maintenance, and subsidies shorten the payback.

Can I pay in EMI?

Yes, through Bajaj Finserv on purifiers, vessels and solar systems.
